Primary Palette

Use one dominant color per state and keep the rest muted.

Usage Rules

Keep the interface predictable.

  • • Apply the monochrome scale to surfaces, badges, banners, buttons and icons—not only text.
  • • Use soft slate tints for large backgrounds and deep slate-900 for primary actions or emphasis.
  • • Rely on contrast, weight and borders to separate hierarchy instead of chromatic accents.

Accessibility

Readable, friendly and inclusive.

  • • Keep text on monochrome surfaces at a contrast ratio above WCAG AA.
  • • Pair dark slate-900 backgrounds with white text and light slate-100 surfaces with slate-800 or slate-900 text.
  • • Pair icons with labels where possible and never rely on shade alone.
